🔥 Drone Kiln

Feed it a song. It slices the audio into overlapping windows, scatters the phase of every frequency bin, and lays the pieces back down far apart — so pitch and timbre survive while rhythm dissolves. Three minutes becomes an hour and a half of standing tone. It measures both ends of that trade, so you can read which properties of the song came through the fire and which burned off.

🪟 Windowing

Each slice is multiplied by a smooth bell before and after transforming, so consecutive grains cross-fade into one another instead of clicking.

🌀 Phase scatter

The magnitude spectrum is kept exactly; only phase is randomised. That is why the chord stays and the drum hit does not. Left and right get independent scatter, which is where the width comes from.

➕ Overlap-add

Grains advance by half a window at the output and by half a window divided by the stretch factor at the input. The ratio between those two numbers is the whole trick.

🧪 Assay

The source is analysed once, frame by frame, when you load it. The drone is analysed live off the same spectrum you hear. Both columns use the same measures, so the difference between them is the kiln.
